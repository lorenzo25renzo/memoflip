About the App

MemoFlip is a mobile flashcard application designed to help students study and remember information easily and enjoyably. Featuring Memo, a friendly brain companion who guides you through your learning journey.

Meet Memo 🧠

Memo is your personal study buddy — always cheering you on, offering tips, and keeping your study sessions fun and motivating. No more stressful cramming!

Key Features

Friendly Brain Guide

Memo keeps you motivated throughout your sessions.

Smooth Card-Flip

Beautiful 3D card animations for a satisfying study experience.

Offline Study Mode

Study anytime, anywhere — no internet needed.

Quick Deck Maker

Create organized decks and cards in seconds.

Clean App Look

A calm, distraction-free design made for focus.

Private User Accounts

Your decks and progress are yours alone.
